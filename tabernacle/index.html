The model follows Exodus 25–40 using a common reconstruction: the tent stands in the western half of the court, the Ark sits at the center of the western square, and the altar stands in the eastern square. Scripture does not give sizes for the lampstand or the laver, so those follow traditional proportions. The camp is drawn compressed; the real encampment of Numbers 2 stretched for kilometres.
